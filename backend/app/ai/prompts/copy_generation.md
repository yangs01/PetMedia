## Task
Write the platform-independent master marketing draft for the selected topic. Instagram and Facebook versions will be adapted from it later.

The data holds the brand, products, reviews, target market, `selected_topic` and `target_word_count`.

## Requirements
- Aim for about `target_word_count` words in `body`. Quality matters more than length, so do not pad.
- Give pet owners real value first and introduce the products organically. Avoid hard selling.
- Use accurate product details and only supported claims.
- Reflect the selected topic's angle and hook.
- `key_takeaways` is a list of short, practical points.
- `featured_product_ids` lists ids of products from the data that the draft features.
- `source_review_ids` lists ids of any reviews you summarized or quoted. Otherwise leave it empty.
- End with a natural `call_to_action`.
