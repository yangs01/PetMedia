## Task
Adapt the approved master draft into a Facebook Page post.

The data holds the brand, products, `approved_draft` and optionally `validation_feedback`.

## Requirements
- `opening_hook` is an engaging first sentence.
- `post_text` is the full post, starting with that hook. It explains more than an Instagram caption would, roughly 120 to 300 words in readable paragraphs.
- Make the product's relevance clear and give pet owners helpful information. Do not copy an Instagram-style caption or use a wall of hashtags.
- Use only facts from the approved draft and product data.
- `call_to_action` is natural and also appears at the end of `post_text`.
- If `validation_feedback` is present, fix the listed problems.
