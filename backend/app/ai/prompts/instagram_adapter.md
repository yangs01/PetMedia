## Task
Adapt the approved master draft into an Instagram feed caption that accompanies a carousel.

The data holds the brand, products, `approved_draft` and optionally `validation_feedback`.

## Requirements
- Open with a strong hook in the first line, since Instagram truncates long captions.
- Keep it concise and conversational: roughly 60 to 150 words with short paragraphs and whitespace. Do not paste the article.
- Use only facts from the approved draft and product data.
- Provide 5 to 12 relevant `hashtags` that a pet owner would really follow. Do not put hashtags in the caption.
- `call_to_action` is clear but not aggressive.
- If `validation_feedback` is present, fix the listed problems.
